# What does property management cost in Columbus, Ohio?

Property managers in the Columbus area price their work as a monthly fee, either a percentage of rent or a flat dollar amount, plus separate one-time charges for finding a tenant and renewing a lease. The ranges below come from nine companies that publish prices on their own websites. They are companies we reviewed, not a market average or a survey, and companies that do not publish prices are not represented.

*General information. Figures captured 2026-11-11 from each company's own site; check the company before relying on them.*

## The fee types

- **Monthly management fee.** Either a percent of rent collected or a flat monthly amount per home or unit.
- **Leasing (tenant placement) fee.** A one-time charge when a new tenant is found, usually a share of one month's rent. See [leasing fees versus renewal fees](/guides/leasing-fee-vs-renewal-fee/).
- **Renewal fee.** A charge when an existing tenant signs a new lease.
- **Minimums and extras.** Dollar floors on percentage fees, onboarding charges, and similar items.

## What the nine companies publish

Of the nine companies, six publish a percentage management fee and three publish flat monthly fees. Eight publish a leasing fee and seven publish a renewal fee. Each company's own page is cited in the table below and in the sources list.

| [Real Property Management Midwest](https://www.rpmmidwest.com/pricing/) | 8.5% of rent collected; page says minimums and maximums may apply | Not published | Not published |
| [Swiss Property Management Group](https://www.swisspmg.com/pricing) | 8% (Core) or 10% (Signature), minimum $100 or $120 | Core: 75% of a month, at least $800. Signature: 50%, at least $660 | Core: one third of a month, at least $500. Signature: 25%, at least $350 |
| [Reafco Ohio Elite Management](https://sites.google.com/view/ohio-elite-management/fees/monthly-management-fee) | 9% of gross rents, or $75 per unit, whichever is greater | One month's rent for a vacant unit | $150 |

In short, among these nine companies the percentage fees run from 6% to 10%, the flat monthly fees from $75 to $145 depending on plan and number of units, and leasing fees from 25% to 100% of a month's rent. These are ranges across a small, self-selected group. A company that hides its prices may charge more or less.

## Percent versus flat, with an illustration

The two models behave differently as rent changes. Suppose, purely as an illustration, a home rents for $1,500 per month:

- A 6% fee is $90 per month and a 10% fee is $150 per month.
- A flat plan at $105 per month costs the same whether the rent is $1,200 or $2,000.

Percentage plans rise and fall with rent, while flat plans stay fixed. This is arithmetic on a stated example, not a prediction of what you will pay. Also compare what each plan covers, since a lower monthly fee may come with larger leasing or renewal charges.

## Published ranges from other sources

These are statements by third parties, separate from our data, and not Columbus-specific.

- HomeGuide says new-tenant placement can run from 25% to 100% of the first month's rent, and that some companies charge an extra $100 to $200 for a lease renewal. For percentage management fees it gives 8% to 12% of monthly rent for single-family homes and for properties with two to ten units, and flat fees of $100 to $150 per month for a single-family home. The page carries no date ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/property-management-fees)).
- Flat Fee Landlord compiled four published guides (checked 2026-10-17). All four put the monthly fee at 8% to 12% of rent, and the page calls these rules of thumb rather than measured averages ([Flat Fee Landlord](https://flatfeelandlord.com/property-management-costs)). Flat Fee Landlord sells its own management plans, so treat it as a vendor.

Our reviewed companies' percentage fees start lower than those third-party ranges. That may reflect who chooses to publish prices, so it should not be read as a local trend.

## What to check in any quote

- Is the percentage charged on rent collected or rent billed?
- Is there a minimum monthly amount?
- What are the leasing and renewal fees, and are there dollar floors?
- Are there setup, onboarding, maintenance markup, or termination charges?
- Does the flat fee apply per home or per property?
