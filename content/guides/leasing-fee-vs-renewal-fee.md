# Leasing fee vs renewal fee: what is the difference?

A leasing fee, also called a tenant placement fee, is a one-time charge when a manager finds and places a new tenant. A renewal fee is a one-time charge when an existing tenant signs a new lease. They are separate line items, and a management agreement can include one, both, or neither.

*General information. Company figures captured 2026-11-11 from each company's own website.*

## The leasing fee

The leasing fee pays for the work of filling a vacancy. HomeGuide describes it as covering advertising, screening, preparing the lease and rekeying locks, and says it can run from 25% to 100% of the first month's rent ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/property-management-fees)). The Flat Fee Landlord roundup of four guides reports that most of them cite 50% to 100% of one month's rent, with some flat-dollar alternatives ([Flat Fee Landlord](https://flatfeelandlord.com/property-management-costs)).

What eight Columbus-area companies publish on their own sites:

|---|---|
| [Evernest (Columbus)](https://www.columbuspropertymanagement.co/pricing) | 50% of one month's rent, $500 minimum |
| [HomeRiver Group](https://purehomeriver.com/locations/ohio/columbus/) | 50% of first month's rent |
| [Mynd](https://mynd.co/locations/ohio/columbus-property-management) | 50% of one month's rent |
| [ROOST Real Estate Co.](https://roostrealestateco.com/monthly-property-management-fees-flat-vs-percentage-based/) | 100% for 1 unit, 75% for 2-19 units, 50% for 20+ units |
| [10X Property Management](https://www.10xpropertymanagers.com/pricing) | 25% to 100% depending on plan; standalone leasing 75% with a $650 minimum |
| [Reafco Ohio Elite Management](https://sites.google.com/view/ohio-elite-management/fees/leasing-fee) | One month's rent for a vacant unit |

| [Swiss Property Management Group](https://www.swisspmg.com/pricing) | Core: 75% of a month, at least $800. Signature: 50%, at least $660 |

Across these eight companies, leasing fees range from 25% to 100% of a month's rent. They are companies we reviewed because they publish prices, not a market average.

## The renewal fee

A renewal fee covers the paperwork and rent review when a tenant stays. HomeGuide notes that many companies fold renewals into their monthly percentage, while others charge an extra $100 to $200 ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/property-management-fees)). The roundup shows wide variation: one of its four guides lists $100 to $350 flat, and another lists 25% to 50% of a month's rent or $200 to $500 ([Flat Fee Landlord](https://flatfeelandlord.com/property-management-costs)).

What seven Columbus-area companies publish:

| Company | Renewal fee as published |
|---|---|
| [Reafco Ohio Elite Management](https://sites.google.com/view/ohio-elite-management/fees/monthly-management-fee) | $150 |
| [Evernest (Columbus)](https://www.columbuspropertymanagement.co/pricing) | $250 |
| [ROOST Real Estate Co.](https://roostrealestateco.com/monthly-property-management-fees-flat-vs-percentage-based/) | $275 |
| [Mynd](https://mynd.co/locations/ohio/columbus-property-management) | $299 |
| [10X Property Management](https://www.10xpropertymanagers.com/pricing) | $200 to $300 depending on plan |
| [NorthPoint Asset Management](https://northpointam.com/pricing) | 25% of one month's rent |
| [Swiss Property Management Group](https://www.swisspmg.com/pricing) | Core: one third of a month, at least $500. Signature: 25%, at least $350 |

Among these seven, flat renewal fees run from $150 to $300 and the rest are a share of a month's rent. HomeRiver Group and Real Property Management Midwest publish no renewal fee on the pages we read, which is not the same as having none.

## How the two fees compare, with an illustration

Imagine, for illustration only, a $1,500 rent:

- A 50% leasing fee is $750. A 75% fee is $1,125.
- A $250 flat renewal fee is $250. A 25% renewal fee is $375.

In the published figures above, each company that lists both fees charges more for a new placement than for a renewal at this example rent. The sums depend only on the example rent and the published rates, not on any prediction of your costs.

## Why the distinction matters when you compare companies

- **A low monthly fee can sit beside a high leasing fee.** Compare the total over a typical year, including how often you expect to re-lease.
- **Long tenancies favor lower renewal fees; frequent turnover favors lower leasing fees.**
- **Minimums matter.** Some published fees use the greater of a percentage or a dollar floor, so a low-rent home may pay more than the percentage suggests.
- **Plans change the numbers.** Where a company lists several plans, leasing and renewal amounts can differ by plan, and we do not always know which value belongs to which plan.

## What to check in a quote

- Is the leasing fee a percent of a month, a flat dollar amount, or the greater of both?
- Is there a fee for a tenant you find yourself?
- Is a renewal fee charged every renewal, or once?
- Does the renewal fee apply to month-to-month extensions?
- Are re-leasing fees owed if the manager is fired mid-lease?
