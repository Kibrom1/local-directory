## Other findings
- Fee data: only 9 companies publish fees on their own pages. No competitor has a sourced, multi-company Columbus fee comparison, so structured fee data is a real gap, but the dataset is small.
- Ohio has no separate property management license (a real estate broker license is required), so the state licensee file cannot isolate property managers; candidates must come from websites.
- Display ads would earn roughly $1.50-$75/month at this traffic, so ads are not a meaningful revenue plan. Affiliate, pay-per-lead and sponsored lists conflict with the no-payments rule.
- Programmatic-page risk: staged rollout of only pages with unique data.
- Caveat: the research shell could not reach most sites, so supply and fee counts are probably understated versus what a browser-based pass would find.

## Recommendation: WIDEN, do not pivot to home services
1. Keep property management as the core, but widen the content to landlord topics (leasing, rent pricing, tenant screening, eviction, fee guides). Landlord topics carry more search volume and use the same page engine.
2. Use the fee data as the differentiator: a Columbus fee guide plus a comparison table of the 9 companies with published fees (with sources and dates).
3. Staged launch: publish only pages with unique data (start with ~30-60 pages), then add as supply grows. Do not publish toward 150 pages with thin data.
4. Continue supply work toward ~40-60 companies with a browser-based, company-by-company pass (robots/terms checked per site) and the Division licensee file once located.
5. Revise thresholds: pages at launch 150 -> 30-60 (staged); companies 60 -> 40; month-12 visits 2,000 -> 600-800; keep the Keyword Planner pull as a gate before the widened content plan is finalized.
6. Do not pivot to home services now: tougher results (Angi, Thumbtack), quote-based pricing weakens the fee-data edge, and the monetization conflicts with the no-lead-gen rule. Keep it as a later narrow extension.
7. Display ads: decide separately; if used, block property-management ad categories.
