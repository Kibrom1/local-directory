## C. Derived from our own per-company data (fee-data.csv, n=9 companies; label as "published by Columbus-area companies we reviewed")
- Management percentage: 6% to 10% (Evernest 6%, HomeRiver 7-10%, RPM Midwest 8.5%, Swiss PMG 8%/10%, Reafco 9%, Northpoint 10%).
- Flat monthly fee models: $75-$145 per door/month (Mynd $109-$129, ROOST $75-$105, 10X $85-$145).
- Leasing fee: 25% to 100% of first month's rent; most common 50%.
- Renewal fee: $150-$299 flat (Reafco $150, 10X $200-$300, Evernest $250, ROOST $275, Mynd $299), or 25% of a month's rent (Northpoint; Swiss PMG greater-of formula).
Caution: small sample, selected because they publish fees; do not present as a market average.

## Not used
BiggerPockets forum threads (user anecdotes, ToS unreviewed), Hemlane/Thumbtack lead-gen city pages beyond items above, Cleveland/Toledo/Cincinnati fee guides (different markets).

## Threshold note (thresholds.md)
Only 9 Columbus companies publish own-page fees. If fee-guide pages are meant to be unique structured data, they must lean on this fee-data set plus the benchmarks above; per-company fee pages should be limited to companies with published figures. Suggest not counting fee-hidden companies toward "pages with unique fee data".
