About Columbus PM Guide

A free, independent guide to property management companies in the Columbus, Ohio area, for rental owners.

Who runs this site

Owner to confirm: person or business entity, contact address. The owner also operates other businesses, including one that serves property managers. That is stated here in plain words; it has no effect on listings, fees or order. SAMPLE wording, owner to approve

What it costs and what it does not

  • Free to use. No accounts.
  • No paid listings, featured placement, referral fees or ads at launch.
  • We do not list rentals, route inquiries or refer tenants.

How we collect data

Facts come from public sources (company websites, public records). Each fact shows its source link, the date we captured it, and the date we last verified it. We do not copy company descriptions or images. Unknown values stay blank.

Corrections and removal

Anyone can correct a fact or remove a listing for free. Correct or remove a listing.
